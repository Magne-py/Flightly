How grading works
Green — on the shortest route & in the right slot.
Yellow — on the shortest route but wrong slot.
Orange — a valid onward flight that still reaches the destination, just via a longer route.
Red — no flight exists from the previous airport, or there's no onward path to the destination.
Light green — the row that completes the journey; lit up when you've finished the puzzle (paler if you took more stops than the shortest route).
